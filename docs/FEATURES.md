# N&T AI-Powered Sustainable Finance & Accounting Platform
## Core Platform Features

### 1. Secure Client Portal
- Secure user authentication
- Organisation-based client accounts
- Role-based access control
- Client and administrator permissions
- Organisation-level data separation

### 2. AI-Powered Financial Insights
- Financial data analysis
- Automated identification of financial trends
- AI-assisted business insights
- Financial risk indicators
- Decision-support recommendations

### 3. ESG Performance Management
- ESG metric tracking
- Environmental, Social and Governance performance monitoring
- ESG reporting dashboard
- Supporting evidence and document management
- ESG performance trend analysis

### 4. Carbon & Energy Management
- Carbon emissions recording
- Scope 1, Scope 2 and relevant Scope 3 data tracking
- Energy consumption monitoring
- Renewable energy tracking
- Carbon performance insights

### 5. Sustainable Finance Management
- Sustainable finance project tracking
- Green finance activity monitoring
- Sustainability-linked financial information
- Project status and progress monitoring
- Sustainable investment data management

### 6. Accounting Workspace
- Accounting work-item management
- Client financial information tracking
- Document and evidence management
- Task/status monitoring
- Centralised client accounting workspace

### 7. AI-Assisted ESG Analysis
The platform is designed to combine financial and sustainability information to support:

- ESG trend identification
- Carbon performance analysis
- Financial and sustainability risk identification
- Automated insights
- Decision-support recommendations

### 8. Multi-Organisation Architecture
Each client organisation is logically separated within the platform.

Client users should only access information associated with their own organisation, while authorised N&T administrators manage the wider platform according to their permissions.

### 9. Future Development
Planned development includes:

- Advanced ESG analytics
- Automated carbon calculations
- AI-generated sustainability insights
- ESG risk scoring
- Sustainable finance opportunity analysis
- Automated reporting
- Client dashboards and benchmarking
